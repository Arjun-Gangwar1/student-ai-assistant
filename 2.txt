GMAIL_CLASSIFIER

Gmail Classifier refers to tools and systems that automatically categorize, label, and organize emails in Gmail using artificial intelligence or predefined rules.
and organize emails in Gmail using artificial intelligence or predefined rules.  These solutions help users manage inbox clutter, prioritize important messages, and enforce data governance policies

n8n Workflow Templates:
Use AI models like Google Gemini, OpenAI, or Claude AI to classify incoming emails.
Workflows can automatically label emails as High Priority, Work Related, Promotions, SPAM, or OFFER, and even delete unwanted messages.

ChatBotKit’s Gmail Classification AI Agent:
 An AI agent that analyzes email content and metadata to assign labels, helping users organize their inbox without manual effort.

No-Code Automation Platforms:
Bardeen AI:
Automates email classification and labeling based on custom criteria. Users define categories (e.g., "Invoices", "Support") and train the classifier with sample emails.

Zapier + MonkeyLearn:
 Classifies new Gmail emails based on subject and body using AI, then applies labels automatically

Google Workspace Data Classification Labels:
Now generally available, these labels (e.g., Confidential, Internal, Sensitive) help organizations manage sensitive information.
Admins can enforce Data Loss Prevention (DLP) policies based on labels (e.g., block emails labeled Confidential from being sent externally).
Users can apply labels when composing or replying to emails
Available in Gmail on the web; mobile support is planned.
Access via Admin Console > Security > Access and data control > Label Manager.

Third-Party Apps & Extensions:

Smart Gmail Data Classifier (Chrome Extension):
Adds classification labels (e.g., Internal, External, Confidential) during email composition. Ideal for teams needing compliance without workflow disruption.
AI Classifier (aiclassifier.tech):
 Offers AI-driven inbox organization with 95% accuracy, custom categories, and productivity insights. Includes a 14-day free trial.
