FEATURES

1. The proposed Smart AI Assistant is designed as a student life operating system, not a single-purpose chatbot.
2. It continuously observes student-related information streams and converts them into actionable, personalized, and predictive intelligence.

# Real-Time Email, PDF & Announcement Intelligence
3. The assistant continuously monitors emails, PDF attachments, and official announcements and performs intelligent understanding in real time.
4. Automatic detection of important academic and administrative emails
5. Filtering of irrelevant or non-applicable announcements
6. Reading and summarizing PDF attachments such as mess menus and circulars
7. Extraction of deadlines, dates, and required actions from unstructured text
8. Priority-based inbox view (high/medium/low importance)
Example:
Instead of searching inboxes for mess menu PDFs, a student can directly ask:
“What is today’s mess menu?”
-----> receive an instant, accurate answer based on the latest document.

# Academic & Classroom Intelligence
9. The system acts as a real-time academic assistant by integrating classroom platforms and course-related updates.
This real-time academic assistant provides quizzes, practice problems for the exams from the content, whatever the student studies in their semester
10. Live tracking of assignments and deadlines
11. Deadline reminders and last-hour alerts
12. Detection of missed submissions
13. Extraction of exam schedules and syllabus changes from notices
14. Noise filtering in classroom feeds to highlight only graded or time-critical updates
Example:
“Assignment deadline for Linear Algebra is in 6 hours. Submission not detected yet.”
----> This proactive guidance helps students act before deadlines are missed.

# Campus Life, Mess & Transport Assistance
15. The assistant simplifies daily campus logistics by continuously monitoring operational updates (power supply & internet updates).
16. Daily mess menu extraction and comparison
17. Bus and transport schedule change detection
18. Hostel and facility notice summarization
19. Emergency or campus-wide alert notifications

Example:
“Bus Route B timing has changed to 7:40 AM starting tomorrow.”
----> This eliminates the need for students to manually search through old emails or notices.

# Events, Opportunities & Career Intelligence
20. Beyond academics, the assistant helps students grow by identifying relevant opportunities.
21. Event and seminar relevance detection based on student interests
22. Eligibility checking for hackathons, internships, and workshops
23. Deadline reminders for registrations and applications
24. Event clash detection and prioritization

Example:
“AI Workshop matches your interest in Data Science. Registration closes tonight.”
----> This ensures students do not miss high-impact opportunities


# Predictive & Decision Intelligence (Key Innovation)
25. Predictive Student Risk & Overload Detection
26. The system continuously maintains a live student state and computes a real-time Student Risk Score using:
27. Number of deadlines in the next 24–48 hours
28. Unread high-priority communications
29. Overlapping assignments and events

Example output:
“High academic overload risk detected. Three deadlines within 24 hours.
---> skip optional event and focus on Assignment X.”

30. Students can interact with the assistant to simulate decisions.

Example query:
“If I attend this seminar, what will I miss?”
System response:
Recomputes workload and deadlines
