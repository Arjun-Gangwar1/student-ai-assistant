1. Use Case & Problem Understanding
1.1 Background & Context

Modern college students operate in a highly dynamic and information-dense environment. Every day, they interact with multiple platforms such as official email, academic portals, classroom systems, document repositories, and messaging groups. These platforms continuously generate streams of unstructured and semi-structured data, including:

Administrative announcements

Mess menus and transport schedules

Assignment notifications and deadlines

Event and seminar invitations

Urgent messages from peers and faculty

At IIT Dharwad, most critical information is disseminated through emails and portals, often in the form of long messages or attached PDFs. This information is time-sensitive, frequently updated, and relevant only to specific subsets of students based on their year, branch, roles, or interests.

1.2 Core Problem

Despite having access to information, students face a serious information overload problem, not an information scarcity problem.

Key pain points include:

Important emails and PDFs getting buried under irrelevant messages

Missed assignment deadlines and event registrations

Manual searching of inboxes and portals for basic queries

Difficulty prioritizing tasks during high-pressure academic periods

Traditional solutions such as static dashboards, calendar reminders, or batch-processed summaries fail because:

They operate on stale data

They lack student-specific personalization

They are reactive, not proactive

1.3 Why Real-Time AI is Essential

Student life evolves continuously:

Mess menus change daily

Transport schedules update unexpectedly

Deadlines expire quickly

Events overlap and clash

Hence, the problem demands a real-time, event-driven AI system that can:

Continuously ingest data as it arrives

Maintain an up-to-date internal state

React instantly to changes

Anticipate student overload before failures occur

This aligns perfectly with Pathway’s philosophy of real-time AI pipelines.

2. Problem Definition & Impact
2.1 Formal Problem Definition

Design and build a real-time personal AI agent for college students that:

Continuously ingests live or realistically simulated data streams

Maintains a live, evolving knowledge base

Filters and prioritizes information using student context

Predicts cognitive and deadline overload

Proactively assists students with actionable recommendations

2.2 Key Challenges Addressed

Multi-source streaming ingestion: Emails, PDFs, portals, classroom updates

Continuous document updates: No batch re-indexing

Personal relevance filtering: Year, branch, interests, roles

Temporal reasoning: Deadlines, overlaps, urgency

Decision support: Helping students choose what to do next

2.3 Real-World Impact

The proposed system:

Saves significant student time

Prevents missed academic and extracurricular opportunities

Reduces mental and decision fatigue

Improves academic performance and well-being

Importantly, it transforms the AI from a passive assistant into an active decision partner.

3. Proposed Solution & Architecture
3.1 Solution Overview

We propose a Personal Predictive AI Agent that continuously monitors student-related data streams and provides real-time, personalized, and proactive assistance.

Unlike traditional assistants, our system does not merely summarize information.
It predicts risk, detects overload, and recommends optimal actions.

3.2 High-Level Architecture

The system consists of the following layers:

Data Sources
Emails, PDFs, college portals, classroom systems, messaging streams

Pathway Connectors (Ingestion Layer)
Real-time connectors ingest data in streaming mode and automatically detect updates

Streaming Intelligence Layer

Parsing and entity extraction

Classification (admin, academic, event, transport)

Personalization using student profile

Deadline and urgency detection

Pathway Document Store (Live Knowledge Base)

Automatic chunking and embeddings

Live vector index maintained inside the pipeline

Continuous synchronization with incoming data

Predictive Risk & Decision Engine (Key Innovation)

Maintains a live student state

Computes overload and deadline risk scores

Performs “what-if” decision simulations

RAG + LLM Layer

Retrieval-augmented generation grounded in fresh documents

Safe, hallucination-free responses

Output & Interaction Layer

Real-time alerts

Daily digests

Proactive recommendations

Natural-language Q&A

3.3 Core Functional Capabilities
Real-Time Information Intelligence

Automatic classification of incoming emails and documents

Detection of relevance based on student profile

Extraction of deadlines, events, and required actions

Live Knowledge Management

No external vector database

No batch re-ingestion

Always-fresh answers using Pathway’s Document Store

4. Innovation & Differentiation (1st-Place Factor)
4.1 Predictive Student Risk Engine (Key Innovation)

Instead of only reacting to events, the system predicts student overload using live data streams.

A continuously updated Student Risk Score is computed using:

Number of deadlines in the next 24–48 hours

Unread high-priority communications

Overlapping assignments and events

Historical behavior patterns

Example output:

“High overload risk detected. Three deadlines within 24 hours. Recommendation: skip optional event and focus on assignment.”

This transforms the assistant into a real-time student decision intelligence system.

4.2 What-If Decision Support

Students can ask:

“If I attend this event, what will I miss?”

The system:

Simulates the impact on deadlines and workload

Recomputes risk scores

Explains trade-offs in natural language

This feature elevates the system from notification-based assistance to decision support AI.
